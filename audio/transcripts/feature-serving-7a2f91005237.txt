Alternatives, evolution, and interview close.

Reject a synchronous full-history warehouse query on every recommendation request: it moves large scans into a tight latency budget. Reject adding a vector database merely because the feature is called AI; ordinary indexed candidate retrieval may satisfy this compact model. Embedding retrieval becomes justified when learned similarity improves candidate quality under measured cost and latency. Reject running a large generative model for this assumed ranking workload without evidence of benefit.

At multi-region scale, replicate relatively stable item features, route user state deliberately, and document freshness during pipeline partitions. Keep model artifacts identical by digest, but allow controlled regional rollouts. Cost includes feature computation, online reads, model execution, experiment logging, retraining, and operational support. Compare benefit with the popular baseline and remove complexity that fails to earn its cost.
