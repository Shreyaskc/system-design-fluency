A short spoken walkthrough.

Candidate: Is the limit a strict global entitlement or regional abuse protection? Interviewer: Begin with regional abuse protection. Candidate: I will start with the simplest durable path and state the boundary before distributing it. Interviewer: Can we simply deploy the same bucket in three regions? Candidate: Only if the contract permits the multiplied allowance. For a strict global budget I would allocate bounded regional rights or coordinate through one owner; the former can strand capacity and the latter adds latency and partition unavailability. I would keep billing quotas in a separate durable accounting path.
