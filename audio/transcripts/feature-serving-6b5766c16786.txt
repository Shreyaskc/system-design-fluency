Exact prompt and scope.

Exact prompt: “Corner Market wants personalized product recommendations. Design the feature and inference serving path, including training consistency, freshness, failure behavior, and safe rollout.” A feature is a measurable input to a model, such as a customer's seven-day category-view count. Inference is applying a trained model to inputs to obtain a prediction. This elective follows analytics because it reuses event collection, replay, and freshness reasoning; it is not a requirement to add artificial intelligence to every system.

What outcome? Assume rank up to 200 eligible candidate products and return 20. Candidate generation chooses a smaller relevant set; ranking orders that set.

What model? Assume a compact C P U-served ranking model, not a large language model. Model training research and graphics-processor infrastructure are outside this interview's scope.

What quality contract? Assume experiments evaluate useful purchases and user experience, with guardrails for errors, latency, stale inventory, and merchant concentration. No guaranteed business uplift is claimed.

Which data? Assume consented product interactions and current catalog eligibility. Never use payment credentials or sensitive personal attributes as features.
