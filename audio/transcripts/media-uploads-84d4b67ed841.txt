Case 4 Reliable listing photos and video . Follow-ups and rubric.

Follow-ups and rubric.

Practice: an upload URL leaks before expiry. Can you call it single use?.

Not by default. The exact storage capability may be reusable while valid. Use short expiry, narrowly scoped keys and permissions, controlled finalization, and explicit provider-supported conditional mechanisms where required. Do not promise revocation that the chosen capability cannot enforce.

Practice: moderation rejects an already popular published file.

Set authoritative status to removed, stop issuing access, purge caches, remove ready references, and record purge completion against the takedown objective. Existing downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Strong: separates accepted bytes from ready media, designs idempotent state transitions, verifies content, and budgets orphan/egress cost. Weak: treats a client completion call or storage event as proof of safety, publishes originals immediately, or assumes a signed URL is single use. Principal depth includes parser isolation, reconciliation ownership, and takedown guarantees.

Technical references checked 2026-10-19: S3 multipart upload; S3 signed URL behavior. AWS is a concrete example; verify limits and checksum behavior for the chosen storage provider.
