A short spoken walkthrough.

Candidate: What should happen if payment succeeds after the hold expires? Interviewer: Do not oversell; refund if stock cannot be obtained safely. Candidate: I will start with the simplest durable path and state the boundary before distributing it. Interviewer: Why not roll back the sequel transaction to reverse the charge? Candidate: The provider is another authority. A local rollback cannot undo its committed effect. I reconcile the stable payment identity, then attempt the permitted order transition.

If inventory cannot be reacquired, I create an auditable refund action with its own retry and reconciliation state, rather than calling the whole workflow atomic.

This is the compact interview explanation. The earlier sections expand the mechanism and failure traces; adapt the time spent on them to the interviewer’s direction.
