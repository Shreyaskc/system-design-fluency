9. Define reliability from the shopper outward . Observe causes and consequences.

Observe causes and consequences.

Metrics are numerical measurements over time; logs are individual event records; traces connect steps across a request. Track request rate, errors, duration, saturation, database lock waits, replica lag, queue age, and business invariant violations. Use stable correlation identifiers without embedding personal data. High-cardinality labels such as raw shopper identifiers can overwhelm metric systems and expose sensitive information. Sample traces carefully so rare errors and slow paths remain diagnosable.

Alert on user impact or imminent loss of a critical promise. A high C P U reading may be healthy utilization; a rising duplicate-capture count deserves immediate attention even when C P U is low. Build runbooks with safe first actions, verification queries, escalation ownership, and rollback instructions. Run a restore exercise and a dependency-outage exercise before claiming recovery readiness. A backup job's success signal is weaker evidence than a verified restoration.
