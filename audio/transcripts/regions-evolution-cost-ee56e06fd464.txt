Recovery objectives are contracts.

R T O states a restoration-time target. R P O states tolerated data loss. Assume an illustrative thirty-minute R T O and five-minute R P O for catalog administration; payment records may require a stricter contract. A backup every day cannot by itself satisfy five-minute R P O. Continuous log shipping might support that goal only if shipping, retention, credentials, restoration, and replay are tested together.

Break recovery into detection, decision, fencing the old writer, data promotion or restore, traffic movement, dependency reconnection, and validation. If these take 3 plus 5 plus 4 plus 6 plus 2 plus 3 plus 4 minutes, total recovery is 27 minutes, leaving little margin under a thirty-minute objective. DNS caching, stuck clients, or missing secrets can consume the margin. Recovery drills should measure the shopper-visible endpoint, not just the moment a database starts.
