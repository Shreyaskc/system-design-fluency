8. Make asynchronous work replayable and bounded . Separate accepting work from finishing it.

Separate accepting work from finishing it.

Asynchronous processing records work for another process to finish later. Corner Market should not fail a committed order because email is temporarily unavailable. A queue distributes tasks to workers; a log retains an ordered sequence that consumers can read independently. Choose a task queue for sending receipts and a retained event log when analytics, search indexing, and audit projections need independent replay. “Asynchronous” does not mean instantaneous, durable, or guaranteed to succeed.

The response must distinguish accepted from completed. Return a stable job identifier and state such as pending, running, succeeded, or failed. Define the maximum queueing delay and how users learn about terminal failure. A worker acknowledgment tells the broker the worker has completed its obligation; acknowledge too early and a crash loses work, acknowledge too late and successful work may be repeated. Exactly which effect counts as completion needs an explicit contract.
