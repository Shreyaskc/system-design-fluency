Glossary precise definitions and practical meaning . T T L.

T T L.

Precise definition: Time to live is a configured validity or retention duration whose exact start point and expiration behavior depend on the protocol or storage system.

Plain English: An entry has a time limit, under specific rules.

Analogy and its limit: A price label marked valid for sixty seconds expires, but replacing it with an already old label does not make the price current.

Concrete example: A cache entry written from a lagging replica can expire sixty seconds after insertion yet describe an older fact. State whether expiration triggers deletion, revalidation, or refusal; T T L alone does not eliminate stale-fill races.
