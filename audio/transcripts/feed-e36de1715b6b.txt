Case 2 A useful neighborhood feed . A short spoken walkthrough.

A short spoken walkthrough.

Candidate: Is the five-second freshness target also a promise to immediately hide blocked content? Interviewer: Freshness may lag, but blocked content must be hidden. Candidate: I will start with the simplest durable path and state the boundary before distributing it. Interviewer: Your inbox already contains the post. Is deleting the source enough? Candidate: No. The inbox is a derived list of IDs, not an authorization authority. I filter visibility at serving time and remove stale references asynchronously. If the authority is unavailable, I omit uncertain content rather than expose it. Feed completeness can degrade while the access boundary holds.

This is the compact interview explanation. The earlier sections expand the mechanism and failure traces; adapt the time spent on them to the interviewer’s direction.
