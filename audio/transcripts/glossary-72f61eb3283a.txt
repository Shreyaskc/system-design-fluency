Glossary precise definitions and practical meaning . Ambiguous outcome.

Ambiguous outcome.

Precise definition: A caller knowledge state in which available observations do not determine whether an operation committed or produced its external effect.

Plain English: No answer does not mean no action happened.

Analogy and its limit: A shopkeeper cannot hear whether a shouted collection request was fulfilled; shouting again may duplicate it. Unlike the analogy, software can preserve a durable request identity.

Concrete example: A provider captures payment but its response is lost. Keep the attempt pending, query or safely retry using its original identity, and reconcile before asserting failure.
