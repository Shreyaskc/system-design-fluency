Lease.

Precise definition: A time-bounded grant of rights whose continued validity depends on a defined expiration and renewal protocol.

Plain English: You own permission for a limited time unless it is renewed.

Analogy and its limit: A market stall rental expires at closing time; the old tenant may still physically remain there. Software pauses likewise do not stop when permission expires.

Concrete example: A worker’s lease expires during a long pause. Another worker becomes owner, but the first can resume executing. Use resource-enforced fencing when stale execution could corrupt shared state, and state clock and timing assumptions.
