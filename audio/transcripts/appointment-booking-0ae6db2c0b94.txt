Case 11 Book a community pickup appointment . Clarification dialogue and requirements.

Clarification dialogue and requirements.

Interviewer: “Can users choose arbitrary durations?” Candidate: “May I begin with fixed, pre-generated, single-capacity slots? Overlapping variable-duration appointments require interval conflict detection.” Interviewer: “Yes. Confirmations must never overlap for a slot.” Candidate: “Then the booking transaction, not the cached calendar, owns that invariant.”.

Assume each clinic offers 100 slots per day, published 90 days ahead. One account can book multiple distinct slots, subject to abuse limits. Cancellation releases capacity; no payment or temporary holds in the first version. Ask about rescheduling: support an atomic move within the same database. Store instants in UTC with the clinic's named time zone for display and schedule generation. A local time can be ambiguous or nonexistent around daylight-saving changes; generate slots using an explicit policy and preserve the resulting instant. The examples are fictional assumptions.

Propose 99.9 percent monthly booking availability and 95th percentile server response below 300 milliseconds under normal regional operation. Successful confirmation means a durable transaction committed. Availability browsing may lag by five seconds; booking always checks current authoritative state. Users receive a conflict response when another person wins. Notifications are asynchronous and do not determine whether the appointment exists.
