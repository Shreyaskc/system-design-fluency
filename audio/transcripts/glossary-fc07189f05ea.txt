Isolation.

Precise definition: The transaction property specifying permitted observations and interference among concurrently executing transactions.

Plain English: It controls how overlapping database operations may affect one another.

Analogy and its limit: Separate checkout desks can share inventory under different coordination rules; physical desk separation alone does not prevent selling the same last item.

Concrete example: Two transactions reading the same stock then writing independently can lose a decrement. Choose an atomic update or appropriate locking and isolation; merely writing BEGIN does not establish every desired guarantee.
